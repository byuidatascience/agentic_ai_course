## Battle of the Bots

**Shelved:** 2026-09-15 — not going to be implemented, as of right now.

**Was:** A capstone competition where students compete against each other for the best agent. Sat
alongside the Contribution Assignment as one of the two capstone projects, and was the concrete
vehicle behind competency 2.2.5 (compare two or more agent versions objectively / pairwise).

**Removed from:**

- `irm.md` — capstone unit row
- `teaching_philosophy.md` — "Content Structure → Capstone"
- `course_outcomes.md` — the inline comment on competency 2.2.5

**What it leaves behind, if it ever comes back or needs replacing:**

1. **The capstone is now one project plus the Contribution Assignment.** `teaching_philosophy.md`
   refers twice to a "self-directed final project" (under Curiosity, and under Final Letter →
   Statement of Accomplishment). With Battle of the Bots gone, that self-directed project is the only
   remaining capstone build and it has no entry in `activities_assessments.md`. It needs defining.
2. **The rubric row changed.** "Main Projects (1-4)" became "Main Projects (1-3)" — P1, P2, P3.
3. **Competency 2.2.5 lost its named vehicle.** Pairwise / A-B comparison is still assessed inside
   Project 2 and practiced in `human-review-definition-practice`, but the capstone-scale version of
   it is gone. `course_outcomes.md` already flags 2.2.5 as not quite capturing what pairwise
   comparison means; that flag is now doing more work than it was.

**Why it was appealing:** it made 2.2.5 vivid, it created peer pressure toward quality at exactly the
point in the semester where motivation sags, and it fit the leaderboard/gamification thread that runs
through Pass-offs. If motivation in the final weeks turns out to be the problem, this is the idea to
reach for first.
